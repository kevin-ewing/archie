# Rook Field barn

Story opens in a quiet barn beside the grass landing field in Flanders. The view looks straight down at an old crate used as a desk. A lamp lights a folded chart, written orders, an envelope, a photograph and a logbook. Each item opens a short example. The orders and letters give the player a sense of the other people at the aerodrome through their writing.

The scene uses Archie's seeded noise, ordered dither, pixel raster and cockpit lettering. The artwork lives in [desk-art.ts](../src/story/desk-art.ts) and the desk options in [barn.ts](../src/story/barn.ts). The options are drafts for future written briefings, correspondence, keepsakes and sortie records.
